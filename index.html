Every grain carries the patience of the Mekong.

From the flooded paddies of the Nine Dragons river delta to the deck of your vessel, we follow the rice through every season — sowing, harvest, milling, packing and loading — so what reaches your port is exactly what was promised.

  1. Gieo trồng · Cultivation

    Crop selected by type, broken rate and moisture to match your specification.

  3. Xay xát & Đóng bao · Milling & Packing

    Milled to grade and packed to your format — bulk, retail or private label.

  4. Xuất cảng · Export

    Loading, documents and shipment followed through to arrival at your port.
