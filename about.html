About Viet Nam Good Rice

Practical rice export capability from the heart of the Mekong Delta.

Viet Nam Good Rice operates from the Mekong Delta area, An Giang Province, where rice supply is abundant, trade flows are active and export work requires speed, discipline and document accuracy.

Who we are

We are an independent rice export marketing and operations desk focused on connecting reliable Vietnamese rice supply with overseas importers, distributors, food service buyers, private-label brands and procurement buyers.

Our strength comes from hands-on rice trading experience: crop seasons, milling reality, export documents, loading pressure, booking risks and buyer communication across multiple markets.

Operating area

Base: Mekong Delta area, An Giang Province, Viet Nam

Source region: Mekong Delta river basin and surrounding rice-growing provinces

Model: Sourcing coordination, specification control, export execution and shipment follow-up

Our promise to buyers

No overclaiming

We communicate what can be controlled and flag what depends on crop, port, carrier or inspection reality.

Document discipline

Invoice, packing list, BL, phytosanitary, CO and inspection files are checked against buyer and customs requirements.

Practical execution

Production, loading, shipment and payment milestones are managed with clear responsibility.

Long-term view

For stable buyers, we standardize product, packing and reporting to reduce repeat-order friction.
