About Viet Nam Good Rice

Practical rice export capability from the heart of the Mekong Delta.

Viet Nam Good Rice operates from the Mekong Delta area, An Giang Province, where rice supply is abundant, trade flows are active and export work requires speed, discipline and document accuracy.

Who we are

An independent rice export desk

We are an independent rice export marketing and operations desk focused on connecting reliable Vietnamese rice supply with overseas importers, distributors, food-service buyers, private-label brands and procurement buyers.

Our strength comes from hands-on rice trading experience: crop seasons, milling reality, export documents, loading pressure, booking risks and buyer communication across multiple markets.
